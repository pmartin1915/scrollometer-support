Privacy Policy

Effective 26 August 2026. Applies to the Scrollometer iOS app.

Scrollometer collects no personal data, transmits nothing off your device, and has no account, no server and no third-party services. The rest of this page explains what that means in detail.

What Scrollometer stores

On your iPhone only, in the app's own storage:

  • Which apps you chose to track, as opaque tokens supplied by iOS. Scrollometer cannot convert these back into app names or read them as text.
  • How many minutes iOS reported for each of those apps, per day.
  • The distances calculated from those minutes, and your settings.

Raw event records are automatically deleted after 8 days. Daily totals are kept so history and weekly recaps work.

What Scrollometer does not do

  • It does not transmit anything. The app contains no networking code.
  • It does not collect analytics or crash telemetry.
  • It contains no advertising SDK and does no ad targeting.
  • It has no account, no sign-in and no server.
  • It shares nothing with third parties, because it sends nothing to anyone.
  • It cannot see the content of any app, your messages, your browsing or your location.

Screen Time access

Scrollometer asks for Screen Time (Family Controls) access during setup. This is used for one purpose only: to receive the amount of time you spent in the apps you selected, so that time can be converted into an estimated distance.

This data is delivered by iOS to Scrollometer on your device and stays there. Apple's framework does not expose what you viewed or did inside those apps to any app, and Scrollometer requests nothing beyond usage time for apps you picked yourself.

You can withdraw this access at any time in the iOS Settings app, under Screen Time.

Data retention and deletion

Everything is stored inside the app's container on your iPhone. Deleting Scrollometer deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing to request, export or have erased — no server ever received it.

Children

Scrollometer collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes and the updated policy is published on this page.
